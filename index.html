I am a research intern at MLAI under the supervision of Prof. Sungju Hwang. I received my Bachelor's Degree of Business Administration and Computer Science at Yonsei University. I worked as a research intern at Language & AGI Lab under the supervision of Prof. Jinyoung Yeo.

My research focuses on developing large language models for real-world applications — AI Agents, decision-making of LLMs.
